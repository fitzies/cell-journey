In the grid, hold a person for a moment, drag, then release to place them. Drag near the top or bottom to scroll. Tap for details. With a keyboard, focus a person and use Alt + arrow keys to move them. Search and Active / Inactive filters still work. Each option has independent sample data. Nothing is saved to the app. The browser approximates the header and tab bar; Expo will retain Apple’s native controls. Content uses Inter Tight with the app’s existing color and spacing tokens.
